Where do the figures in this letter come from?
  • The letter cites a recently published study estimating the deal's cost at over £44.7 billion, projecting around 229,000 excess deaths by 2036 from reduced NHS spending (rising to ~291,000 if adult social care effects are included).
  • These are the campaigner-supplied figures reproduced in the template. Please check the underlying source and satisfy yourself of the claims before sending — and feel free to edit or soften any figure you are not comfortable asserting.
  • MP contact details come from the official UK Parliament Members API. If an email address isn't published there, use the WriteToThem button, which reliably delivers to any MP.
